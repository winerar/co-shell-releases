Privacy policy

What is stored

Transactions, accounts, categories and budgets are stored on your phone. The app works offline and needs no account.

Family cloud (optional)

If you turn on “Family”, shared data syncs through Google Firestore, only for the members of your family. Sign-in uses your Google account; we store its identifier and your member name.

What else goes online

  • Exchange rates: the app downloads public rates; your transactions and amounts are not sent.
  • Feedback: what you wrote, files you attached yourself (for example, a receipt sample), the app version, the phone model, a random installation number and the sync status. Transactions and amounts are not attached.
  • Cloud analysis (only if you turn it on): the text of a note or receipt and the names of your categories and accounts go to an AI server to be parsed and are not stored. Account balances and transaction history are not sent.
  • Invitation code (closed test): the code and a random installation number are stored in our database to count phones per code; the app checks now and then whether the code is still valid.
  • Updates: the app checks whether a new version is out and downloads it from GitHub; the optional “AI on this phone” pack comes from there too, if you install it.

What we do not do

We show no ads, do not sell or share your data with third parties, and collect no analytics about your spending.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS only. You can lock the app with a PIN or fingerprint and hide amounts. Only family members can reach the family cloud data.

Your rights

You can delete your data at any time: see the “Delete data” page.
