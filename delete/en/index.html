Delete your data

Data on your phone

Uninstall the app and the data on the phone is gone. Or tap “Erase all data” in “More → Backup”.

Family cloud

  • “More → Family → Leave family”: you stop seeing shared data; it stays on your phone.
  • “More → Family → Delete family” (family owner): shared data is removed from the cloud for every member.

Cannot delete?

Write to us from the app: “More → Feedback”. Say which data to delete; we will reply and delete it within a reasonable time.
